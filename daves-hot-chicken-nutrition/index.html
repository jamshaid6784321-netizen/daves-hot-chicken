How to read the spice ranges

Dave's Hot Chicken nutrition changes a little with heat, because the hotter spice bands carry more of the chili-oil blend. The pattern is steady across the chart: a tender or slider gains about 60 calories from the mildest band to the hottest, the 10 pc Bites gain about 100, and Dave's #1 gains about 120. Protein never moves with spice. Our spice levels guide explains what separates the bands.

    Cutting

    Order the 10 pc Bites on their own: 650 to 750 calories and 41g of protein, the best ratio on the menu. Dave's #5 (930 to 990, 31g) is the lightest proper combo. A tender with kale slaw instead of fries lands at 760 to 820 calories with 27g of protein, and swapping Dave's Sauce for the 25-calorie Spicy Buffalo side protects the deficit.

    Bulking

    Dave's #2 is the bulk order: 1,500 to 1,620 calories and 68g of protein in one tray. Add a Mac & Cheese (290 calories, 14g protein) before reaching for a shake. A regular Top-Loaded Fries adds 1,390 calories and 36g of protein on its own, which is a meal pretending to be a side.

    Maintenance

    Two tenders ordered on their own, no combo, come to 980 to 1,100 calories and 50g of protein: a full meal that still leaves room in the day. Dave's #4 (1,060 to 1,110 calories, 37g protein) is the combo version of the same idea.

    High-protein day

    Stack the Bites (41g) with a single tender (25g) and you are at 66g of protein for roughly 1,140 to 1,300 calories, before any fries touch the tray.

    Shakes & Drinks: Not on the 2026 Chart

    The 2026 Dave's Hot Chicken nutrition chart covers food only. Shakes, slushers and drinks are not listed on it, and Mini Sliders and the Big Trio have no rows in the published chart either.

    The shake numbers that do exist come from Dave's earlier published chart, revised June 2024, and they are labelled that way wherever they appear: a small shake at 590 to 610 calories, a regular at 740 to 760, and a large at 1,110 to 1,140, with loaded toppings adding roughly 180 to 490 more on a large. Treat those as last-published figures, not 2026 chart values, and expect the current chart to replace them when Dave's updates it.

    How to Order a Lighter Meal

    Dave's Hot Chicken nutrition leaves real room to maneuver if you order deliberately. These are the swaps that move the numbers most, in the order they are worth making.

    Pick Bites over a big combo

    The 10 pc Bites with no fries land at 650 to 750 calories with 41g of protein. Dave's #2 costs 1,500 to 1,620 for the same satisfaction.

    Swap the dip, keep the habit

    A Spicy Buffalo side is 25 calories against 760 for a large Dave's Sauce. That single change saves more than skipping the fries does.

    Drop or split the fries

    Fries add 440 calories and 1,200mg of sodium to every combo. Splitting one portion across the table halves the damage; the kale slaw swap cuts it to 270 calories and 170mg of sodium.

    Eat the slider open-face

    Leave half the bread behind and the slider's 41 to 42g of carbs fall with it. And stay in the lower spice band: Dave's Hot Chicken nutrition adds up to 120 calories at the hot end of the chart.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How many calories are in a Dave's Hot Chicken tender?

    A single tender has 490 calories at the Not Hot to Lite Mild spice band and 550 at Mild to Reaper, with 25g of protein, 29 to 35g of fat and 30 to 31g of carbs. It is the most protein-efficient single chicken item after the Bites.

    Does the spice level change the calories at Dave's?

    Yes, a little. Dave's Hot Chicken nutrition is printed in two spice bands, and the hotter band adds about 60 calories to a tender or slider, about 100 to the 10 pc Bites and about 120 to Dave's #1. Protein, sodium ranges and carbs barely move; the difference is the extra chili-oil blend.

    Which Dave's Hot Chicken item has the most calories?

    Counting shareable boxes, the Hot Mozz Hot Box at about 9,500 calories. For a single serving, the large Top-Loaded Buffalo Mac leads at 2,960 calories, ahead of the 5 pc Hot Mozz at 2,330 to 2,350 and Dave's #2 combo at 1,500 to 1,620.

    What is the lowest-calorie combo at Dave's Hot Chicken?

    Not Chicken #5, the cauliflower tender combo, at 790 to 830 calories. The lowest chicken combo is Dave's #5, one tender with fries, at 930 to 990 calories.

    How many calories are in Dave's Sauce?

    It depends entirely on the size, which is why single numbers mislead: a 0.8 oz packet is 150 calories, a 1.5 oz side is 270, a 1.5 oz dipper cup is 290, and a 4 oz large is 760. Always match the number to the container in front of you.

    How many calories are in a Dave's Hot Chicken shake?

    Shakes are not on the 2026 Dave's Hot Chicken nutrition chart. Dave's earlier chart, revised June 2024, listed a small at 590 to 610 calories, a regular at 740 to 760 and a large at 1,110 to 1,140, before toppings.

    Is Dave's Hot Chicken keto-friendly?

    Not really. A single tender alone carries 30 to 31g of carbs from its breading, a slider 41 to 42g, and fries 58g, so even the plainest order passes a full day of strict keto carbs. The cauliflower items are not a fix either, at 38 to 55g of carbs.

    Where do these nutrition facts come from?

    Every figure comes from Dave's published Nutrition Information chart, foods revised 16 March 2026, as explained in the source note on this page. Dave's Hot Chicken nutrition values are reference figures for standard builds; individual restaurants prepare food by hand, so small variations are normal.
