Dave's Hot Chicken Catering: Hot Boxes & Bundles

Yes, Dave's does catering, and it sells it two different ways: Hot Boxes ordered direct from the store under the name Feed A Crowd, and larger delivery bundles sold through ezCater. The boxes, the sizes and the prices are not the same in the two channels, which is why Dave's Hot Chicken catering prices look contradictory at first glance. Both routes carry the full Dave's Hot Chicken catering menu, and every serves-and-cost figure is worked from real listed prices, never an invented 'average price' range.

Two Ways to Order Dave's Catering

Dave's Hot Chicken catering runs through two channels, and choosing between them is the first real decision: the same chain sells 10-piece boxes for pickup at the store, and 20-piece bundles with fries for delivery through ezCater.

Direct: Feed A Crowd

Order Hot Boxes from your store's regular menu, in the ordering system or in person, and pick them up. Boxes run 10 pieces (40 for Bites), official location pages put the starting options at $30, and heat can be set the way it is on the normal menu. Best for: parties you are already driving to, game day, smaller groups.

ezCater: Feed a Crowd Bundles

Dave's official storefronts on the ezCater marketplace sell named bundles built for delivery: 20 pieces plus fries, listed as serving 10 to 20 guests, with up to two heat levels per bundle, plus individual Box Lunch meals. Delivery minimums, fees and change rules apply and vary by store. Best for: offices and events that need the food to arrive.

Neither channel publishes a national Dave's Hot Chicken catering price, in either direction: a bundle that costs one figure in Florida costs more in California, on the same platform, in the same week. The prices section below works through real examples with their cities attached, which is the only honest way to print them.

Catering Prices: Per Location, By Design

Dave's Hot Chicken catering prices are set store by store in both channels, so the useful question is not "what does it cost" but "what does it cost per guest, in a real city, on a real listing". Worked from the bundle table above:

Hot Box #3, the mixed bundle, listed at $202 in Coral Springs and $220 in Fresno. Spread across the full 20 guests Dave's lists it for, that is roughly $10 to $11 a head; across a hungrier 10, it is $20 to $22 a head. Hot Box #2 runs $225.50 to $250 for the same serves range, so roughly $11 to $25 a guest on the same maths. On the direct side, one New York store's ordering prices, tracked by menu sites from Dave's own system, put the Tenders Hot Box at $50, the Sliders box at $70, the Bites box at $30 and a Box of Fries at $10, which is why official pages can honestly say catering options start at $30. Any site printing one national average for these boxes has averaged away the only thing that matters, which is your city.

Delivery adds its own layer on ezCater: sampled store policies show a delivery minimum of $50, delivery fees from $30, order changes allowed up to 24 business hours ahead, and a 25 percent cancellation fee inside 24 hours. Those figures are one store's listing, not a chain policy, and they are printed here as the shape of what to check on your own store's page before you commit a date.

How to Order, Step by Step

The same five decisions, in the order they actually bite, whichever Dave's Hot Chicken catering route you take.

1. Count guests, honestly

Meal or grazing? A working lunch eats more per head than a party with other food. Count, then pick the serves figure from the bottom half of the range if it is a meal.

2. Pick the route

Pickup and flexible timing: direct Feed A Crowd boxes. Delivery, a set arrival time and individual meals: ezCater bundles and Box Lunches. Both are Dave's Hot Chicken catering; only the route differs.

3. Set the heat for a crowd

ezCater bundles allow up to two heat levels per bundle; direct boxes follow the normal menu. A crowd splits safest as a Mild-to-Medium base with one Hot box for the brave, and no Roulette unless the group is in on it.

4. Cover the sides and sauces

Boxes are chicken-first. Add the Box of Fries or the Mac & Cheese Bundle, kale slaw for a fresh counterweight, and sauce in the 4 oz large size rather than a pile of packets.

5. Order ahead, and read your store's terms

Catering is not a walk-in order. Place it ahead, and check your store's own minimums, delivery fees and change window on its listing page; the 24-hour change rule on sampled ezCater stores is typical of what you will find.

6. Add the vegetarian box

Cauli Slider and Cauli Bites Hot Boxes exist in both channels. One cauli box per ten guests covers the vegetarian seats without a separate order elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Dave's Hot Chicken offer catering?

Yes, two ways. Stores sell Feed A Crowd Hot Boxes (10-piece boxes of tenders, sliders, bites and more) for pickup from the regular menu, and Dave's Hot Chicken catering also runs through official ezCater storefronts selling Feed a Crowd Bundles and Box Lunch meals for delivery. Official location pages put the starting catering options at $30.

How much does Dave's Hot Chicken catering cost per person?

From real listings: the ezCater Hot Box #3 bundle at $202 to $220, serving 10 to 20, works out at roughly $10 to $11 per guest at full stretch and $20 to $22 per guest for a hungry ten. Direct boxes run lower per box (a tracked New York store lists the Bites box at $30 and the Tenders box at $50). Your city's prices will differ; catering is priced per store.

How many people does a Dave's Hot Box feed?

A direct 10-piece box feeds about 4 to 5 guests at two pieces each, with fries ordered on top. The ezCater bundles are listed by Dave's as serving 10 to 20, because they carry 20 pieces plus four orders of fries. Plan from the bottom of the range when the box is the meal.

Can you choose different spice levels in a catering order?

Yes. ezCater bundles allow up to two heat levels per bundle, and direct boxes follow the normal menu, where heat is set per piece. All seven levels are available; keep a crowd order centred on Mild and Medium, and remember the Roulette box hides one Reaper piece in ten.

Does Dave's catering deliver, or is it pickup only?

Both exist. Direct Feed A Crowd boxes are a pickup format from your store. Delivery catering runs through Dave's ezCater storefronts, with per-store delivery minimums (a $50 minimum on sampled listings), delivery fees and change rules set by that store's page.

How far in advance should you order Dave's catering?

Ahead, not same-day walk-in. Sampled ezCater store policies allow changes up to 24 business hours before the order and charge 25 percent for cancellations inside 24 hours, which tells you the planning horizon the system expects. For direct boxes, order through your store's system the day before at the latest for a large count.

Is there a minimum order for Dave's Hot Chicken catering?

For direct pickup boxes, no published minimum beyond the box itself. For ezCater delivery, yes: sampled stores set a $50 delivery minimum, and delivery fees start around $30. Both figures are per-store settings, so confirm them on your store's listing before you build the order around them.

Are there vegetarian catering options at Dave's?

Yes. The Cauli Slider Hot Box and Cauli Bites Hot Box run in the direct lineup, and cauliflower bundles appear on ezCater too. One honest note: the cauliflower is breaded and fried like the chicken (it is the vegetarian line, not a gluten free or certified vegan one), which the allergen and gluten free guides on this site cover in full.
