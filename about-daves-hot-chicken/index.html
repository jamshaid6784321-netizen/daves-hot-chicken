About Dave's Hot Chicken: The Guide Behind the Menu

About Dave's Hot Chicken is the guide behind the menu on this site — an independent record of the full Dave's Hot Chicken menu, checked item by item, so the price and the calories on the page match the counter. About Dave's Hot Chicken covers the categories, the price ranges and the calorie figures the chain publishes, in one place.

What the Guide Is

An independent menu guide for Dave's Hot Chicken, the Nashville-style hot chicken chain. The guide is not affiliated with the chain, not endorsed by it, and not a channel for ordering — orders go through the chain's own app and stores.

The guide answers a single question: how much does a Dave's order cost, and how many calories does it carry? The answer needs more than a screenshot — prices vary by store, the menu changes, and the official nutrition chart is revised from time to time. The guide keeps the answer in one place, updated when the sources move, and every figure on the site traces back to a named source: the official chart, a live store listing, or a named third-party listing such as ezCater or GiveX.

The site is organised in three parts. The menu itself carries the nine categories in short cards. The guides cover one question each — hours, spice levels, nutrition, halal, allergens, gluten, rewards, catering, the gift card, promo codes, the secret menu, the franchise cost, the Fallout meal and the sauce recipe. And the store directory lists every store on a map and by state. About Dave's Hot Chicken holds the three parts together: the same figures, the same sources, the same plain style.

What the Guide Covers

The menu comes first: all nine categories — combos, singles, bites, cauliflower, sides, shakes, hot boxes, drinks and sauces — with prices as reference ranges and calories for the standard build. The Dave's Hot Chicken menu page carries the full list in short cards, each with its image, price and calories, and the item popup adds the nutrition for the item.

Beyond the menu, the guide covers the hours for every store, the seven spice levels from Not Hot to Reaper, the full nutrition chart, the Frequent Fryer rewards program, catering hot boxes and the gift card. The store directory is the second half of the guide: the Dave's Hot Chicken locations page lists 267 stores, with addresses, phone numbers and published hours, on a map that sorts them by distance. Stores that are announced but not open yet are marked opening soon, and two stores are listed as permanently closed, so the count stays honest about what is trading and what is not.

About Dave's Hot Chicken also covers the questions around the menu: halal, allergens, gluten, promo codes, the secret menu and the franchise cost, each on its own guide page. The Dave's Hot Chicken hours guide covers the times — the typical pattern, the late-night exceptions and the holidays.

How Prices, Calories and Hours Are Checked

Calories and nutrition come from the official nutrition chart, revised 16 March 2026, checked against live ordering menus. Where the chart gives a range, the guide prints the range; where a figure is estimated — the spice-level calorie step, for example — the estimate is labelled as one. The guide treats the chart as the single source for calories: no figure is invented, and no figure is carried over from an older chart without a label.

Prices are reference ranges from checked US listings, and prices vary by store, so the guide prints a range and says so where the range is wide. Hours come from Google listings for each store, with the official website as the second source where the two disagree — the listing that is confirmed wins. Catering prices come from the chain's ezCater storefronts, and the gift card facts come from the GiveX portal, the only place the card is sold online. Where a store publishes no phone number or hours, the guide says so instead of guessing.

When two sources disagree, the guide follows the confirmed one and says so. A price on a live ordering menu outranks a price on a listing page; a time on a Google listing outranks a time on an old menu PDF. Figures that cannot be confirmed are left out rather than printed with a guess. That is how the guide keeps its figures honest: a printed number either comes from the chart or is labelled as an estimate.

Who the Guide Is For

First-timers use the guide to pick a heat level and a combo before they reach the counter. Regulars use it to check the calories on a usual order, or to see whether the price at their store has moved. Travellers use the store directory to find the nearest open store and its hours. Anyone with a dietary question — halal, allergens, gluten — finds the chain's own position stated plainly, with the limits of that position stated too.

About Dave's Hot Chicken is written for all four, in the same plain style: short cards, checked figures, no filler. The guide is independent. Dave's Hot Chicken owns the brand, the recipes and the restaurants; the guide owns only its own words, its own checks and its own images. Nothing on the site is an official announcement, and nothing here takes an order or a payment. Corrections go through the contact page, and a correction that checks out against the sources is applied.

The guide is free to read, with no account, no sign-up and no paywall.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is About Dave's Hot Chicken the official Dave's Hot Chicken website?

No. About Dave's Hot Chicken is an independent guide, not affiliated with Dave's Hot Chicken, the restaurant chain. The chain's own site and app are the only official channels; the guide covers the menu, prices, calories, hours and stores, and links nowhere to the chain's ordering pages.

Where do the prices and calories on the site come from?

Calories and nutrition come from the official nutrition chart, revised 16 March 2026. Prices are reference ranges from checked US listings, and hours come from Google listings for each store. About Dave's Hot Chicken prints a range where the sources give one, and labels every estimate as an estimate.

How many stores does the guide cover?

267 unique stores, from 268 published listings — one store appears twice under two names and is counted once. About Dave's Hot Chicken lists all 267 on a map and in a directory grouped by state, with 115 marked as opening soon.

Does the guide take orders or sell gift cards?

No. About Dave's Hot Chicken takes no orders and no payments. Orders go through the chain's own app and stores, catering through ezCater, and the gift card through the GiveX portal — the guide explains each route and links to it where the chain's own listing allows.
