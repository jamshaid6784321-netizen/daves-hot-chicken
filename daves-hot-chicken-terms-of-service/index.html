Using the Guide

Dave's Hot Chicken Menu Guide is a free menu guide: the full menu with prices and calories, plus hours, spice levels, nutrition, rewards and store locations, in one place.

No account is needed and no sign-up is required. The guide reads on any device, and every page works without payment, registration or personal details. Use of the guide is governed by the Dave's Hot Chicken terms of service, and continued use means acceptance of those terms. The guide is written for readers planning an order: check the Dave's Hot Chicken menu, check the total, check the nearest store, then order through the chain's own channels.

Prices, Hours and Calories

Prices, hours and calories on the guide are reference ranges from checked listings, not live figures from the till.

A price on the guide is the range found across checked US listings for the standard build of the item. The exact price at a store depends on the location, the format and the day, and a store can change a price without notice. The same applies to hours: the Dave's Hot Chicken hours on the guide come from published listings, and each store sets its own opening and closing times. Before travelling or ordering, confirm the price and the hours with the local store — the Dave's Hot Chicken locations page carries the address and phone number for every listed store.

Calories come from the chain's published nutrition figures, revised 16 March 2026, with estimates marked as estimates where the chain publishes no figure. A calorie figure is for the standard build of the item; sauces, add-ons and a different spice level change it. Nothing on the guide is a medical or dietary recommendation, and the allergen and nutrition pages carry the detail behind every figure. The Dave's Hot Chicken terms of service treat every figure on the guide as a reference range, and the local store is the final word.
