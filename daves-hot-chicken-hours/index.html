Dave's Hot Chicken Hours: Opening and Closing Times

Dave's Hot Chicken hours follow a simple pattern at most locations: doors open at 10 AM or 11 AM, most kitchens close at midnight, and Friday and Saturday run later. There is no single chain-wide schedule, though, because every store sets its own times. This guide covers Dave's Hot Chicken hours properly: the typical opening and closing times, the late-night exceptions, holiday hours, and the fastest way to confirm the exact times for your store before you go.

Typical Dave's Hot Chicken Hours

Dave's Hot Chicken hours at most restaurants start at 10 AM or 11 AM and end at 12 AM. Friday and Saturday nights run later, usually to 1 AM or 2 AM, and a small number of stores stay open until 2:30 AM or 3 AM. A few locations close earlier, at 11 PM, mostly early in the week.

What Time Does Dave's Hot Chicken Open?

Dave's Hot Chicken opens between 9 AM and 11 AM depending on the store, and 10 AM is the most common opening time.

Most locations keep the same opening time all seven days, so a store that opens at 10 AM on Monday opens at 10 AM on Saturday too. Takeout-focused stores can start earliest: the Los Angeles takeout location on S Central Ave opens at 9 AM. Stores in quieter markets start at 11 AM, like the Tampa location above.

If you are planning a lunch run, the safe window is from about 10:30 AM onward, when every format of store is open. Weekend opening times rarely change, but the closing times do, which is where most people get caught out.

What Time Does Dave's Hot Chicken Close?

Most locations close at 12 AM (midnight) on weeknights. On Friday and Saturday the close moves to 1 AM or 2 AM at most stores.

Dave's Hot Chicken hours stretch later than most chicken chains. Late night is part of the brand's identity: official store pages carry a whole section on late-night hot chicken, and weekend closing times stretch to match. The latest closes we found in store listings reach 2:30 AM, and a handful of stores run to 3 AM on their busiest nights. At the other end, some stores close at 11 PM early in the week, so a midnight assumption can leave you at a locked door on a Monday.

One practical tip: plan to place your order at least 15 to 20 minutes before the listed closing time. Kitchens wind down at the end of the night, and the last orders of the day go in before the doors officially close.

Is Dave's Hot Chicken Open Now?

The fastest way to know is to check Dave's Hot Chicken hours on your own store's listing, because only that listing reflects today's hours at your location.

Searches like "Dave's Hot Chicken near me now" usually mean one thing: is the kitchen still taking orders tonight? Three checks give you a certain answer in under a minute:

1. Your store's own page

Find your location in the brand's store locator and open its page. The day-by-day hours list there is the one the store itself publishes.

2. The map listing

Your store's map listing shows a live open or closed status and today's closing time, which is handy on holidays and weekends.

3. Delivery apps, with care

Delivery apps run their own hours and often stop taking orders before the store itself closes. An app saying closed does not always mean the kitchen is closed.

Dave's Hot Chicken Holiday Hours

Dave's Hot Chicken does not publish a chain-wide holiday schedule, so holiday hours are decided by each store.

That is why no honest page can print one table of Dave's Hot Chicken hours for the holidays that fits the whole chain. On major holidays, individual locations may open late, close early, or stay closed for the day, and two stores in the same city can choose differently. Fan sites that print exact holiday hours for every store are guessing.

The same rule covers every big holiday on the calendar: Christmas Day and Christmas Eve, Thanksgiving, New Year's Day, Easter, Memorial Day, the Fourth of July, Labor Day, Halloween, Black Friday, Veterans Day, Columbus Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Good Friday, Valentine's Day and St Patrick's Day. On all of them, the store sets its own hours, so confirm before you travel.

The reliable routine is simple: check your store's listing a day before the holiday, or call the store and ask. If you are ordering for a group on a holiday, our catering guide explains the group-order options that work when a store is open.

Why Hours Differ by Location

Dave's Hot Chicken is a franchise chain, and each restaurant sets hours that fit its market, its format, and local rules.

Franchise ownership

Local owners run most stores, and each owner sets the Dave's Hot Chicken hours for their own restaurant. Two stores an hour apart can keep completely different hours.

Store format

Takeout-focused locations can open earlier and run later than full dine-in restaurants, as the 9 AM to 2:30 AM Los Angeles takeout store shows.

Local demand

Stores near nightlife and college areas stay open for the late crowd. Quieter suburban stores close earlier on weeknights.

Site rules

A store inside a mall or food hall follows the building's hours, which can cut both the morning and the night short.

Once you know your own store's pattern, it rarely changes outside of holidays. And whenever you do visit, the spice levels guide will help you pick your heat before you reach the counter.
