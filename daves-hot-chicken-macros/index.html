Dave's Hot Chicken Macros: Protein, Carbs and Fat by Item

Dave's Hot Chicken macros, in one line: a single tender carries 25g of protein, 30 to 31g of carbs and 29 to 35g of fat, and the 10 pc Bites are the protein heavyweight of the menu at 41g. This page breaks down Dave's Hot Chicken macros item by item from the official nutrition chart (foods revised 16 March 2026), ranks the menu by protein per calorie, shows the macro split of each big item, and builds orders for cutting, bulking and maintenance out of the real numbers.

Read the pattern, not just the rows: this is a fat-led menu. Even the leanest item, the Bites, takes roughly half its calories from fat, because everything is breaded and fried and the spice blend is oil-based. The Bites win on protein share (about a quarter of their calories), the cauliflower items collapse on protein share, and fries are a carb item wearing a side's clothing. Any Dave's Hot Chicken macros plan has to be built around that fat share, not in denial of it.

Why the 10 piece Bites win

The 10 piece Bites pack 41g of protein into the menu's most efficient package because almost everything on the tray is chicken: there is no bun, no fries are bundled in, and the breading-to-meat ratio is lower than a slider's build. On Dave's Hot Chicken macros alone, they are the most macro friendly order on the menu, and it is not close.

Tender or slider for protein?

The slider wins the headline (31g against 25g) and ties on efficiency, but the extra protein arrives with 11g more carbs, because the slider is a full build: bun, slaw, pickles and sauce around the same chicken. If carbs are your tight budget, the tender is the cleaner protein; if total protein per order matters more, take the slider.

Cutting

Order the 10 pc Bites on their own: 650 to 750 calories and 41g of protein, the best ratio on the menu. Dave's #5 (930 to 990, 31g) is the lightest proper combo. A tender with kale slaw instead of fries lands at 760 to 820 calories with 27g of protein, and swapping Dave's Sauce for the 25-calorie Spicy Buffalo side protects the deficit.

Bulking

Dave's #2 is the bulk order: 1,500 to 1,620 calories and 68g of protein in one tray. Add a Mac & Cheese (290 calories, 14g protein) before reaching for a shake. A regular Top-Loaded Fries adds 1,390 calories and 36g of protein on its own, which is a meal pretending to be a side.

Maintenance

Two tenders ordered on their own, no combo, come to 980 to 1,100 calories and 50g of protein: a full Dave's meal that still leaves room in the day. Dave's #4 (1,060 to 1,110 calories, 37g protein) is the combo version of the same idea.

High-protein day

Stack the Bites (41g) with a single tender (25g) and you are at 66g of protein for roughly 1,140 to 1,300 calories, before any fries touch the tray. The Bites Hot Box, built for a group, carries about 165g of protein across its 40 pieces.

One more line for the log book: the Saucy Bites meals run 670 calories (Buffalo), 810 (Spicy BBQ) and 920 (Hot Honey) before sides. Full calorie detail for every meal sits on the nutrition page; the goal cards above are where Dave's Hot Chicken macros turn into an actual order.

Carbs at Dave's: The Honest Picture

Carbs are where Dave's Hot Chicken macros bite hardest. The breading alone puts 30 to 31g on a plain tender before anything else is added, and the fries in every combo carry 58g more.

The slider's 41 to 42g against the tender's 30 to 31g is the bun, slaw and sauce build, which is why the standard low-carb move at Dave's is the slider eaten open-face: leave half the bread behind and the carbs fall with it. The lowest-carb full order on the chart is a single tender at 30 to 31g, or a tender with kale slaw (8g of carbs) instead of fries. Cauliflower is not the escape it looks like: Cauli Bites carry 53 to 55g of carbs, more than the chicken Bites, because the breading does the talking.

And the keto question, answered plainly: no item at Dave's is keto-friendly. The plainest thing on the menu, one tender, spends 30 to 31g of carbs in a single sitting. Low-carb Dave's is possible; keto Dave's is not.

How Spice Changes Your Macros

Heat moves Dave's Hot Chicken macros in exactly one direction: protein never changes with spice, but the hotter bands add calories and fat through the chili-oil blend, about 60 on a tender or slider, 100 on the Bites and 120 on Dave's #1.

For logging, that means the entry you pick should match the band you ordered: a Reaper tender logged as a Not Hot tender under-records by about 60 calories and 6g of fat. The spice levels guide shows what separates the bands; this page's ranges already assume you order across them.

Sauces: The Macro Trap

Dave's Hot Chicken macros change more at the dip cup than anywhere else on the tray. Dave's Sauce is a fat delivery system: even the small packet is 150 calories and 16g of fat with no protein at all, and the sizes climb hard from there.

Logging Dave's in a Tracking App

A warning before you search your app: many Dave's Hot Chicken macros entries in tracking apps and the figures repeated on Reddit come from older data, and the nutrition info copied from them is out of date. A tender listed at 449 calories or 37g of protein predates the current chart; the real tender is 490 to 550 calories and 25g of protein.

Log the chart values from this page as your reference sheet instead: pick the end of each range that matches the spice band you ordered, and accept a small variance either way, because the food is breaded and portioned by hand in each kitchen. To total a full order before you split it, the calorie calculator on our homepage adds up the tray; this page then gives you the protein, carb and fat split to log against it. Dave's publishes no national prices, so protein-per-dollar math is deliberately left out of these Dave's Hot Chicken macros: the grams are official, the prices are not.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much protein is in a Dave's Hot Chicken slider?

31g of protein, with 620 to 680 calories, 41 to 42g of carbs and 37 to 43g of fat. The slider carries more protein than a tender (25g) because it is a full build, but it also carries about 11g more carbs.

How much protein is in two Dave's tenders?

50g: each tender has 25g of protein, and spice level does not change it. Ordered as Dave's #1 with fries, the same two tenders are listed at 56g of protein for the whole meal, 1,230 to 1,350 calories included.

What has the best protein-to-calorie ratio at Dave's?

The 10 pc Dave's Bites: 41g of protein for 650 to 750 calories, about 5.9g of protein per 100 calories. Tenders and sliders tie next at about 4.8g, and fries sit at the bottom at about 1.4g.

Is Dave's Hot Chicken high in protein?

For fast food, yes: the chicken items run 25 to 41g of protein each, and Dave's Hot Chicken macros beat most fried-chicken menus on protein per calorie. The honest caveat is the split: roughly half the calories come from fat, and sodium runs 1,210 to 3,630mg across the meals.

What should I order at Dave's on a cut?

The 10 pc Bites on their own (650 to 750 calories, 41g protein) or Dave's #5 (930 to 990 calories, 31g). Swap fries for kale slaw, swap Dave's Sauce for the 25-calorie Spicy Buffalo side, and stay in the lower spice band to keep the numbers at the bottom of their ranges.

Does the spice level change the macros?

Only the energy side. Protein is identical at every heat level; the hotter spice bands add about 60 calories to a tender or slider, 100 to the Bites and 120 to Dave's #1, through the chili-oil blend. Dave's Hot Chicken macros are printed as ranges for exactly this reason.

Is Dave's Hot Chicken keto-friendly?

No. A single tender has 30 to 31g of carbs from its breading, a slider 41 to 42g and fries 58g, so even the plainest order passes a strict keto day's carbs. Dave's can fit a low-carb day at a stretch; it cannot fit keto.
