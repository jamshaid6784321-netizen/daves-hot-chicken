What the Policy Covers

Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy covers the Dave's Hot Chicken Menu Guide — the independent menu guide at davehotchicken.us. The guide is not affiliated with Dave's Hot Chicken, the restaurant chain, and nothing on it comes from the chain.

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

The policy covers what data the guide collects when the menu, the store locator, the calorie calculator and the guides are used, and what data it never collects. Reading the guide needs no account, no sign-in and no payment. The Dave's Hot Chicken menu and every guide on it open without asking for a name, an email address or any other detail. Data is collected only in the situations below: cookies, the contact form, and the standard server logs that record a visit.

The guide is written and maintained independently. Prices, hours and calories are checked against the chain's published listings and the store listings, and corrections arrive through the contact form. None of that checking needs personal data from the reader.

Cookies, Including Advertising Cookies

Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy treats cookies plainly: a cookie is a small file a browser stores so a site or a service can remember something between visits. The guide itself runs no account system, so no cookie on it holds a login, a password or a profile.

Advertising cookies appear when ads run. The guide plans to show Google AdSense advertising, and AdSense sets cookies to choose and measure the ads shown. Those cookies belong to Google and follow Google's advertising rules, including the option to switch off personalised ads in Google's ad settings. Until ads appear, no advertising cookies are set. The Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy sets no advertising cookies itself; it only describes the ones AdSense sets.

Embedded services set their own cookies too. The store map comes from Google Maps, and opening the map can set Google's cookies. Links out to ezCater, GiveX, the app stores and the social channels leave the guide entirely, and each of those services applies its own privacy rules from the moment the link is followed.

The cookies on the guide fall into two groups. First-party cookies would come from the guide itself; the guide sets none that track a visitor, because there is no account to remember. Third-party cookies come from the services embedded in the page — advertising and the map — and those services set them under their own rules.

Cookie controls sit in the browser, not in the guide: every modern browser clears cookies, blocks third-party cookies, or switches off personalised advertising. Blocking cookies does not block the menu, the guides or the store locator — the guide reads the same with cookies off.

Server Logs

The server that delivers the pages keeps the usual access logs: the page requested, the time of the request, the browser type, and the address the request came from. The logs exist to keep the site running and to spot broken links and failed pages.

The Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy counts server logs as the third and last kind of data the guide collects. The logs are not used to identify individual visitors, not combined with contact-form details, and not shared with advertisers. They are technical records of the site working, nothing more.

How Long Data Is Kept

Contact-form details are kept only as long as the answer needs them. Once a message is answered and any correction from it is applied, the details serve no further purpose and are not kept as a list, a file or a record to revisit.

Server logs are kept for the ordinary working life of the site's hosting, and cookies live only as long as the browser keeps them or the service that set them allows. Nothing on the guide is archived as a personal record.

What the Contact Form Collects

The contact form is the one place the guide asks for personal details: a name, an email address and a message. Those details go only to answering the message — a correction to a price or an hour, a broken link, or a question about the guide. The details are not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not added to any mailing list, because the guide runs no mailing list.

A message sent through the form is read, the correction behind it is checked against the guide's own sources, and the guide is updated where the correction holds. That is the whole life of a message under the Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy: read, checked, answered, and nothing further.

A message about the restaurants themselves belongs elsewhere. The guide cannot take orders, change a booking or answer for a store — the store locator lists the phone number of each store for that. Privacy questions go the same way as any other message: through the Dave's Hot Chicken contact page.

What the Guide Never Collects

No accounts exist on the guide, so no passwords, no usernames and no order histories are stored. No payment is taken anywhere on the guide, so no card numbers or billing details pass through it. No comments section exists, so no comment text or commenter details are stored either.

That list is the point of the Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy: the guide is built to work without holding personal data. Location is the one apparent exception, and it is not one. The map's "Use my location" button asks the browser for the visitor's position, and the position is used only to sort the nearest stores by distance. The position is not stored, not sent to the guide's server and not shared; closing the page ends it.

No analytics tool is running on the guide at the time of writing. No visit counter, no audience report and no advertising measurement runs until ads appear, and the advertising cookies described above arrive only with the ads themselves. If an analytics tool is added later, the policy is updated first, and the date at the top of the policy moves with it.

Third-Party Services and Links

Several parts of the guide run through other services. The store map runs on Google Maps. Catering orders go to ezCater. Gift card purchases and balance checks go to the GiveX portal. App downloads go to Google Play and the App Store. Social links go to TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Each of those services collects its own data under its own privacy policy, and Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy does not cover what happens after a link is followed or a service opens. The guide links to those services because they are the places the chain itself uses; the guide does not control them, and a question about what one of them collects belongs to that service.

The guide shares nothing with those services about individual visitors. No list of readers, no contact-form details and no browsing history leaves the guide for a third party. Each service sees only its own side of a visit — a map load, a link followed, an ad shown — under its own rules.

Children's Privacy

The guide is a general menu guide, written for anyone reading a menu. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13, and the contact form is not aimed at children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal details through the form can raise it through the Dave's Hot Chicken contact page, and the details are removed.

The rule is simple: no collection, and removal on request.

Removal Requests

Anyone who sent details through the contact form can ask for them to be removed. The request goes through the Dave's Hot Chicken contact page, naming the message and the details to remove.

The details are removed, and the removal is confirmed in the reply. Removal covers the message itself; a correction already applied to a price or an hour stays applied, because the correction is a fact about the menu, not a personal detail.

Changes to the Policy

Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy is updated when the guide changes — when ads start, when a service is added or removed, or when the contact form changes. The date at the top of the policy shows the last update.

Checking the date is the fastest way to see whether anything has moved since the last visit. No notice is sent about a change, because the guide holds no email list to send one to; the updated policy is the notice. The policy carries no version numbers and no changelog beyond that date, so the date is the whole record of what changed and when.

Privacy Questions

Privacy questions go through the Dave's Hot Chicken contact page, the same route as any other message to the guide. No email address is published on the guide, so the contact form is the route. A question about data, cookies or removal of contact-form details is answered the same way as a correction: the message is read, and the guide is fixed or the details removed where the request checks out.

There is no other route.

Frequently Asked Questions

What data does the Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy cover?

Three things: cookies, the details sent through the contact form, and the standard server logs that record a visit. The Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy covers nothing else, because the guide runs no accounts, takes no payments and keeps no comment section.

Does the Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy use cookies?

Yes, through advertising and embedded services. Google AdSense cookies appear when ads run, and the Google Maps store map can set Google's cookies. The guide itself sets no login or profile cookies, because there is no account system for one to remember. The cookie picture is advertising and embedded services only.

How does the Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy handle children's data?

It does not collect it. The guide is a general menu guide and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent details through the contact form can ask for removal through the contact page.

How do I ask a privacy question under the Dave's Hot Chicken Privacy Policy?

Through the Dave's Hot Chicken contact page. No email address is published on the guide, so the contact form is the route for questions about data, cookies or removal of contact-form details.
