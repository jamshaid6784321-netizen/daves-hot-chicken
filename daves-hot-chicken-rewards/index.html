Dave's Hot Chicken Rewards: How Frequent Fryer Works

Dave's loyalty program is called Frequent Fryer, and its core rate is set: 10 Dave's Frequent Fryer Points for every dollar you spend, recorded whether you order online, in the app or in store. Around that core sit the tiers, the birthday question and the expiry rule.

What Is the Frequent Fryer Rewards Program?

The Dave's Hot Chicken rewards program is Dave's Frequent Fryer Points: a free loyalty program inside Dave's official app and ordering system that pays points on Dave's own orders and trades them back for free food.

The basics: the program runs in the official app; you get 10 points for every dollar you spend (at participating stores, restrictions apply); points are recorded whether the order is placed online, in the app or in store; and the app carries app-only offers and early access to new items and collabs. New members get a welcome offer: download the app and a regular fountain drink is free (U.S. only). The exact tiers, the birthday question and the expiry window follow, exactly as they stand for members. Does Dave's Hot Chicken have a rewards program worth the two minutes signup takes? On the numbers alone, a free drink and roughly a tenth of your spend back in food: yes, and a good return for two minutes.

How to Sign Up and Get the Free Drink

Joining Dave's Hot Chicken rewards happens in the official app or through Dave's own ordering sign-in; there is no card to buy and no fee to join.

1. Get the official app

Dave's app is published by Dave's Hot Chicken Franchise Co. on Google Play and the App Store. The app is where points, offers and redemption live.

2. Create the account before you order

Points attach to the account on the order. An order placed as a guest, or at the till without the account, is an order the program never sees.

3. Claim the welcome drink

The welcome offer: download the app and get a free regular-sized fountain drink, U.S. only. It is the one reward every member starts with, before a single point exists.

Dave's Hot Chicken rewards in the app and the web account are the same program: points are recorded across online, in-app and in-store orders, so the account you make in the app is the one your signed-in web orders feed too.

How to Earn Points

Points are recorded on online, in-app and in-store orders. To make sure of it: be signed in for app and web orders, and identify the account at the till for counter orders.

In store, the routine is a QR scan from the app or the phone number on the account, given before payment; the points follow the order. Ordered at the counter and forgot? The barcode on the receipt can be scanned afterwards to claim the points; the window for that is measured in hours, so the safe routine is simple: scan the same day, and treat any later claim as a favour, not a right.

The hard boundary: third-party delivery orders earn nothing. A DoorDash or Uber Eats order never touches the Dave's account, so no Dave's Hot Chicken rewards points are recorded on it, and no points can be spent there either. The program only sees orders Dave's itself takes.

How to Redeem a Reward

Dave's Hot Chicken rewards are spent from inside the app: pick the reward your points cover, and the app presents it for the order, as a scan or a Dave's Hot Chicken rewards code at the till, or applied inside an app or web checkout.

The working rules: one reward per order, a redemption limit of once per day, and no combining a reward with other promotional discounts on the same order. The Dave's Hot Chicken 6 digit reward code is the same mechanic: the code the app shows for a chosen reward when you redeem at a register. It is generated per member, per reward, inside the app; it is not a public coupon, and there is no shared code to copy from a website, which is the whole subject of the promo code guide. Check your Dave's Hot Chicken rewards balance in the app before building an order around a tier; the balance in the app is the counter that counts.

Rewards Not Working? The Common Fixes

Almost every Dave's Hot Chicken rewards not working case — the long Reddit threads included — comes down to the same handful of causes, in the order they usually turn out to be true:

"Your selected reward is not valid for this order"

The error (dave's hot chicken your selected reward is not valid for this order) means the reward and the basket do not match: the item the reward covers is not in the order, a reward was already used on it, or the store does not run that offer. Add the exact item the reward names (the slider is the menu's sandwich, in everything but name), once, in Dave's own app.

Points missing after a visit

The order never met your Dave's Hot Chicken rewards account: guest checkout, no scan, no phone number. The receipt-scan route is the recovery; same day is the safe window.

A named offer will not apply

A dave's hot chicken gold reward, a dave's hot chicken silver reward and a dave's hot chicken gold reward not working all point at named in-app offers. Their contents and terms sit inside the app, on the accounts they are issued to; the offer's own text is its terms. Check the conditions and expiry inside the app, and that you are signed into the account it was issued to.

One more cause sits under a share of Dave's Hot Chicken rewards code complaints: codes are member-specific and single-use, generated at redemption. A code from a website, a screenshot or another member is not broken; it was never yours. The support route for anything beyond that is the contact page on daveshotchicken.com.

Birthday Rewards and Point Expiry

Does Dave's Hot Chicken have birthday rewards? No. No birthday reward is part of the program: no free slider, no shake, no drink for birthdays.

So, does Dave's Hot Chicken give birthday rewards? There is none to count on. A birthday offer in the app, in the birthday month, is a member offer — live when it appears, and absent by default. The Dave's Hot Chicken birthday reward pattern on Reddit is exactly that: some members, some years, an offer; no standing promise. Expiry runs on the same terms: points lapse after about 12 months of account inactivity. The working habit is to spend points on the cycle the tiers set — a free item every few visits — rather than banking a balance for a year. A Dave's Hot Chicken challenge reward sits in this section too: the Reaper carries none. The Reaper is a spice level with a waiver, not a rewards mechanic.

Dave's Day and App-Only Offers

The app carries app-only offers and early access to new items and collabs. On top of that sits the program's annual peak: Dave's Day, October 13, with a free chicken slider claimed through the app.

Dave's Day lives in the app: check there as October 13 approaches. Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Dave's Hot Chicken Frequent Fryer program work?

It is the free Dave's Hot Chicken rewards program, run through the official app and Dave's own ordering. The rate: 10 Frequent Fryer points for every dollar you spend at participating stores, recorded on online, in-app and in-store orders. Points trade back for free food at the set tiers: 300 points for a drink or side, 600 for a shake, cheese fries, slider or tender, and 1,200 for a free combo.

Does Dave's Hot Chicken have a rewards program?

Yes: Dave's Frequent Fryer Points, inside the official app. It is free to join, pays 10 points per dollar on Dave's own orders, and starts with a confirmed welcome offer, a free regular fountain drink for downloading the app (U.S. only).

How many points do I need for a free meal at Dave's?

On the tier table, 1,200 points, which at the 10-points-per-dollar rate means about $120 of Dave's orders. The 1,200-point reward is a free combo (#1 to #4). At a typical $13 to $15 combo order earning 130 to 150 points a visit, that is roughly eight or nine visits.

Do Dave's Hot Chicken points expire?

Points lapse after about 12 months of inactivity; no finer expiry rule is published. Spend on the cycle the tiers encourage, a free item every few visits, and the question never becomes practical.

Do I earn points on DoorDash or Uber Eats orders?

No. Points are recorded on orders Dave's itself takes: online, in the app and in store. Third-party delivery orders never touch your Dave's account, so they earn nothing and rewards cannot be spent on them.

What if I forgot to scan my account in store?

The barcode on your receipt can be scanned afterwards to claim the points; the window is measured in hours and no exact figure is published, so scan the same day and treat the claim as time-sensitive.

Can I use more than one reward on an order?

No: one reward per order, redemption once per day, and rewards do not combine with other promotional discounts on the same order. Split the treats across visits instead; the points keep.

Is there a Dave's Hot Chicken birthday reward?

No — there is no Dave's Hot Chicken rewards birthday offer in the program today. If an offer appears in the app in your birthday month, it is a member offer; anything beyond that is rumour.
