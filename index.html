Why People Love Dave's

Fresh Never Frozen

The chicken is fresh, never frozen, so the breading fries up crisp instead of soggy.

24-Hour Marination

Each piece marinates for a full day before it is breaded and fried, which is where the flavour under the heat comes from.

The Secret Sauce

Dave's Sauce goes on sliders and comes alongside tenders: creamy, tangy, and the fastest way to cool a Hot order down.

Dave's Hot Chicken vs. Top Competitors

Most chicken chains spread themselves across a long menu. The Dave's Hot Chicken Menu keeps it short: tenders, sliders, bites and cauliflower, all built around the same seven-level heat ladder, so the choice is the heat, not a page of options.

💡 Why Do Dave's Hot Chicken Prices Vary?

Dave's stores are franchised and each store sets its own prices, so the same combo costs more in a big-city store than in a small-town one. That is why every price on this page is a checked range, not a single figure: confirm your store's price in the official app before you order.

Dave's Hot History

Dave's started in Los Angeles in 2017, in East Hollywood, built around one idea: Nashville-style hot chicken, done at seven heat levels. From that single LA start it has grown across the US and beyond, to London in the UK and Toronto in Canada.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest Dave's Hot Chicken combo?

Dave's #4, one slider with fries, at $10.49–$11.89 in the listings checked for this Dave's Hot Chicken Menu guide.

How many spice levels are there?

Seven, from No Spice to Reaper. The Reaper level needs a signed waiver before you can order it.

What are Dave's Hot Chicken hours?

Most stores open late morning and close between 11 PM and 1 AM, later on weekends. Hours are set per store, so check your local store before a late run.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Yes. The Dave's Hot Chicken Menu includes a cauliflower menu that mirrors the chicken one: cauli sliders, cauli tenders and cauli bites, all with the same spice levels. See the Cauliflower tab above.

Why do prices change from store to store?

Stores are franchised and set their own prices, so this guide shows checked ranges instead of one price. Your store's exact price is in the official app.

Can I get Dave's for a group?

Yes. Hot Boxes of sliders, tenders or bites are the party format, listed in the Hot Boxes tab above.
