How Finding a Store Works

Three steps take you from the full list of Dave's Hot Chicken locations to the counter of the right store.

1. Search or share your location. Type a city, state or ZIP code, or press "Use my location". The map recentres on that place.

2. Compare the nearest stores. The list under the map sorts Dave's Hot Chicken locations by distance and shows what each store publishes: street address, phone number and, where the store has released them, its hours.

3. Check the pin, then go. Tap any pin for the same details on the map itself, and use the directions button for routing. If a store is tagged opening soon, it is announced but not trading yet, so pick an open store nearby instead.

Before You Go

Two things about Dave's Hot Chicken locations are worth knowing before you travel: hours are local, and not every listed store is open.

Hours differ by store. The chain is franchised, so each restaurant sets its own opening and closing times. Most open at 10 AM or 11 AM and close at midnight, later on weekends, but your store's own listing is the only exact source. The hours shown with each pin are the hours that store publishes; where a store publishes none, this page shows none rather than guessing.

Opening soon means not yet. 115 of the Dave's Hot Chicken locations in the official locator are announced stores that have not opened. They usually publish an address first, then a phone number, and hours last. On the map they carry yellow pins, and in the directory below they are tagged, so an announced store is never mistaken for an open one.

Phone numbers come from the listings. 234 of the 267 stores publish a phone number. If you need to confirm a large order, a holiday opening or a halal question, calling the store beats any guide, including this one.

Where the Stores Are

Dave's Hot Chicken locations are concentrated in the chain's home state and a handful of big metros, with a growing list overseas.

California holds 67 Dave's Hot Chicken locations, more than double any other state. Texas follows with 28, New York with 16 and Massachusetts with 11. The deepest single city is Toronto with five stores, level with Los Angeles, and Brooklyn alone has four, which is why Brooklyn gets its own guide on this site. Most states sit in the one-to-eight store range: the chain is national, but it is still densest within a short drive of its Los Angeles start.

Outside the USA, Dave's Hot Chicken locations run in Canada (six Ontario stores), Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar, plus the United Kingdom. The international stores are in the directory below under their country, with the same published details as the US stores.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Dave's Hot Chicken locations are there?

There are 267 unique Dave's Hot Chicken locations. The chain's official store locator publishes 268 listings, but one Plano, Texas store appears twice under two names with the same address and phone number, so it is counted once here. The count covers open stores and announced stores together: 115 are marked as opening soon, so the number already trading is smaller and changes as new stores launch.

How do I find the Dave's Hot Chicken location near me?

Use the map on this page. Type your city, state or ZIP code into the search box, or press "Use my location" and let your browser share where you are. The map then lists the nearest Dave's Hot Chicken locations in distance order, each with its address, phone number, published hours and a directions button.

Which state has the most Dave's Hot Chicken locations?

California has the most Dave's Hot Chicken locations by a wide margin, with 67 stores. Texas is second with 29, then New York with 16 and Massachusetts with 11. The chain started in Los Angeles, and California is still its home ground.

Why do some Dave's Hot Chicken locations have no phone number or hours listed?

Because the store has not published them yet. Most of the listings without a phone or hours are opening-soon stores: the address is announced before the store trades, and the phone and hours follow closer to opening day. On this page a missing phone or hours means the official listing does not carry one, not that it was left out.

Are new Dave's Hot Chicken locations opening soon?

Yes. The official locator currently marks 115 Dave's Hot Chicken locations as opening soon, almost half of all listings. They appear on the map and in the directory below with an opening soon tag, and most publish an address before they publish a phone number or hours.

What are the hours at Dave's Hot Chicken locations?

Hours are set store by store, because the chain is franchised. The usual pattern is a 10 AM or 11 AM opening and a midnight close, later on Friday and Saturday, and each store's own listing is the exact source. Our hours guide walks through the typical pattern and the late-night exceptions.
