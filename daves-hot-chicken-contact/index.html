What the Form Is For

Dave's Hot Chicken Contact covers three things: a correction to a price, an opening time or any other detail on the guide; a link on the guide that no longer works; and a question about the guide itself.

A correction works best with the details attached: the page it sits on, the item or store it concerns, and what the right figure is. A price correction lands faster with the store and the price seen there; an hours correction lands faster with the store and the day it applies to. The guide is checked against the listings the chain publishes, so a correction that checks out goes live and a correction that does not is left alone. That check is the whole point of Dave's Hot Chicken Contact: the guide only changes on evidence, never on a single report.

A broken link is worth the same treatment: the page it sits on and where it was supposed to lead. Links on the guide point to the guide's own pages, to store listings, and to the few outside pages the guide names, like the ordering and catering pages; a link that lands nowhere is fixed once it is reported.

A question about the guide is the third use: how a figure was reached, where a listing came from, or what a section means. A question about a price or a figure is answered from the same listings the guide is built on. Questions about the restaurants themselves — their hiring, their franchising, their plans — belong with the restaurants, not with the guide.

Send a Message

Fill in the form and send it. A topic helps the message land in the right place; the message itself is the part that matters.

The form sends through FormSubmit. No account is needed, and nothing from a message is published.

What the Form Is Not For

The form reaches the guide, not the restaurants. Orders, complaints about a visit, and anything a store has to fix go to the restaurant's own channels, not to this page.

For a store's phone number, the Dave's Hot Chicken locations page lists every store with the number the store publishes. For an order, the restaurant's own app and ordering pages are the route; the guide takes no orders and holds no order details. A message sent here about an order is read, but it cannot be answered with anything a store would do. Dave's Hot Chicken Contact is for the guide only, and the line between the two is kept on purpose.

The guide can fix its own pages and nothing else. It cannot change a store's hours, add a store, or settle a bill; those sit with the store and the chain. A report that a store's listing is wrong is still useful — the listing on the guide is the part that gets fixed.

After You Send a Message

Every message sent through Dave's Hot Chicken Contact is read. A correction is checked against the listings before anything changes, and the guide is updated when the correction checks out.

There is no promised timeframe: a correction sent through Dave's Hot Chicken Contact goes live once it checks out, and a question is answered by a fix to the guide where one is needed. A message with no page, no item and no detail to check cannot be acted on, so the details in the form are what make the difference.

Questions are answered the same way: by a fix to the guide where one is needed, not by a reply that restates the page. The form asks for an email address so a correction that needs a follow-up question can get one; the address is used for that and nothing else.

Check the Guides First

Most questions are already answered on the guide. The full menu with prices and calories is on the Dave's Hot Chicken menu page, the Dave's Hot Chicken hours page carries opening and closing times store by store, and the Dave's Hot Chicken locations page carries every store with its phone number and published hours.

A message that repeats a page is still read, but the answer is already on the page it repeats. The form is for what the guides do not cover, or cover wrong.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Dave's Hot Chicken Contact work?

Fill in the form with a name, an email address and the message, then send it. The form covers corrections to prices, hours and other details, broken links, and questions about the guide. A topic on the form helps the message land in the right place; the message itself is the part that matters.

Does this form reach Dave's Hot Chicken restaurants?

No. Dave's Hot Chicken Contact reaches the guide only, not the restaurants. Orders and complaints about a visit go to the restaurant's own channels, and store phone numbers are on the locations page of the guide.

What should a correction include?

The page it sits on, the item or store it concerns, and what the right figure is. A price correction lands faster with the store and the price seen there; an hours correction lands faster with the store and the day it applies to. A message with no page, no item and no detail to check cannot be acted on.

How long until a correction goes live?

There is no set timeframe. A correction sent through Dave's Hot Chicken Contact is checked against the listings first, and the guide is updated once it checks out. A correction that does not check out is left alone.
